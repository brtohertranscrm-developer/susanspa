## Susan Spa & Resort

**Document type:** Website Design Direction  
**Purpose:** Revamp website and digital booking experience  
**Status:** Working design direction, ready for audit and implementation  
**Last updated:** September 2026

---

## 1. Role of This Document

This document is the visual and experience direction for the Susan Spa & Resort website.

It must be read before designing or modifying any page, component, copy, image treatment, animation, or booking journey.

This document defines the intended identity and experience. It does not replace verified business data, room information, rates, policies, availability, contact details, or legal content. Those must come from the current authoritative source.

When this document conflicts with verified operational data, verified operational data wins. When a design decision is not covered here, choose the option that best supports clarity, calm, trust, and direct booking.

---

## 2. Brand Context

Susan Spa & Resort is a highland resort and wellness destination in Bandungan, Semarang, Central Java. The property is positioned around mountain scenery, fresh air, relaxation, accommodation, spa experiences, family stays, romantic escapes, weddings, and private events.

The property sits approximately 1,100 metres above sea level on the slopes of Mount Ungaran. Its visual character is shaped by:

- Mountain and valley views
- Cool highland atmosphere
- Lush tropical vegetation
- White architecture and warm terracotta roofs
- Spa and wellness experiences
- La Kana Chapel and destination wedding potential
- Rooms, suites, and villas for different stay occasions
- A sense of retreat from the city

### Verified current property themes

The current official website communicates:

- Susan Spa & Resort as a leading spa destination in Java
- A location in Bandungan, Semarang
- Mountain scenery from approximately 1,100 metres above sea level
- Spa and rejuvenation experiences
- Rooms, suites, and villas
- La Kana Chapel
- Family and leisure facilities
- Proximity to attractions such as Gedong Songo, Ayanaz, Celosia, and Saloka

Any additional claim must be verified before publication.

**A refined highland sanctuary where mountain scenery, wellness, meaningful stays, and memorable celebrations come together.**

### Brand promise

Guests should feel that Susan Spa & Resort offers a genuine pause from everyday routines, supported by a distinctive mountain setting and experiences worth travelling for.

### Differentiators to communicate

1. A recognisable highland location and panoramic setting
2. Spa and wellness as a central experience, not a decorative add-on
3. A property suitable for leisure stays, family escapes, romantic occasions, and celebrations
4. La Kana Chapel and the resort setting as a meaningful wedding destination
5. Multiple room, suite, and villa options for different guest needs

Do not communicate differentiators using unsupported superlatives such as "the best", "number one", or "most luxurious" unless evidence and approval are available.

## 7. Creative Concept

### Concept name

**A Sanctuary Above the Everyday**

The visual system should create the feeling of arriving at a retreat set above daily routines. The mountain landscape provides scale and breathing room. The architecture provides recognition. Human details provide warmth. The booking journey provides confidence.

### Visual narrative

The website should move through five emotional stages:

1. **Arrival:** reveal the highland setting and sense of escape
2. **Discovery:** help guests understand rooms and experiences
3. **Connection:** show real human moments, spa rituals, dining, family time, and celebrations
4. **Confidence:** provide practical details, policies, availability, and verified proof
5. **Decision:** make booking or enquiry easy and direct

---

## 8. Design Dials

### ENERGY: 2

Calm and composed with selected moments of visual impact. The property photography should create the strongest emotional impression.

### RHYTHM: 2

Consistent structure with deliberate variation. Alternate wide landscape moments, editorial text layouts, room showcases, practical information, and focused conversion sections.

### MOTION: 1

Subtle and functional. Use restrained transitions, image reveals, hover feedback, and navigation state changes. Avoid parallax-heavy storytelling, looping decoration, floating objects, and animation that delays booking.

### Design read

**Reading this as a highland wellness resort for leisure guests, families, wedding couples, and event organisers, using a refined editorial and nature-led visual language with ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, and MOTION 1.**

## 9. Visual Principles

### Photography leads

The property, landscape, rooms, spa, chapel, and genuine guest experiences are the primary visual assets. Interface decoration must remain secondary.

### Nature frames the architecture

Use mountain, garden, mist, foliage, sky, and light as compositional elements. Do not cover important architecture with text or decorative overlays.

### Quiet confidence

Create hierarchy through scale, typography, photography, spacing, and composition. Do not rely on glow, heavy shadow, oversized badges, or unnecessary gradients.

### Warmth within calm

The design must not become sterile. Introduce warmth through terracotta accents, ivory backgrounds, tactile materials, authentic people photography, and considered editorial composition.

### Clarity supports luxury

Room details, booking information, prices, inclusions, venue specifications, and policies must be easy to find. Luxury must never be used as an excuse for vague navigation or low-contrast text.

## 10. Colour System

The palette is inspired by the resort's mountain setting, white architecture, terracotta roofs, and misty atmosphere.

### Core colours

- **Forest:** `#1D2A23`  
  Primary text, dark surfaces, navigation on light backgrounds

- **Warm Ivory:** `#F4F0E7`  
  Main warm background and editorial sections

- **Stone:** `#D8D1C4`  
  Borders, subtle surfaces, and quiet dividers

- **Terracotta:** `#A45138`  
  Primary accent and selected conversion moments

- **Sage:** `#788473`  
  Secondary natural accent and supporting surfaces

- **Mist:** `#899A9A`  
  Secondary atmospheric accent

### Functional colours

- **White:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Charcoal:** `#20231F`
- **Success:** `#3F7353`
- **Warning:** `#A66B2F`
- **Error:** `#A33F37`
- **Information:** `#3F6674`

### Usage ratio

- 55% warm ivory and white
- 25% forest and charcoal
- 15% photography-led natural tones
- 5% terracotta or another approved accent

### Colour rules

- Use a maximum of three core colours and one accent on a single page.
- Terracotta is an accent, not a colour for every icon, link, border, and heading.
- Do not use blue-purple gradients, neon colours, rainbow gradients, or luminous technology-style effects.
- Avoid pure black as the dominant background unless required by a specific photographic composition.
- All text must meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Never place white text directly over an unpredictable bright image without a tested overlay.

The values above are the proposed digital palette. Before final release, compare them with the official logo, signage, printed collateral, and approved brand colours. Replace proposed values with authoritative brand values when supplied.

## 11. Typography

### Direction

Use an editorial serif for emotional headlines and a clean humanist sans-serif for navigation, body copy, forms, prices, and operational information.

### Recommended characteristics

**Display serif:**

- Elegant but readable
- Moderate contrast
- Warm and established
- Suitable for destination and hospitality storytelling
- Avoid overly decorative wedding-script styles

**Body sans-serif:**

- Highly readable on mobile
- Open forms and comfortable spacing
- Friendly rather than technical
- Strong numerical legibility for rates and dates

### Candidate combinations

Final font selection requires licensing and performance validation. Suitable candidates include:

- Cormorant Garamond with Source Sans 3
- Lora with Manrope
- Fraunces with Source Sans 3
- Libre Baskerville with Inter, only when Inter is chosen deliberately for operational clarity

### Type scale

- Display hero: `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 6.5rem)`
- H1 internal page: `clamp(2.25rem, 4.5vw, 4.75rem)`
- H2: `clamp(1.8rem, 3vw, 3.25rem)`
- H3: `clamp(1.3rem, 2vw, 2rem)`
- Body large: `1.125rem` to `1.25rem`
- Body: `1rem` minimum
- Supporting text: `0.875rem` minimum

### Typography rules

- Use sentence case for headings.
- Avoid long all-capital headings.
- Small uppercase labels are permitted only when they improve navigation or categorisation.
- Body line length should generally remain between 55 and 75 characters.
- Do not centre long paragraphs.
- Do not use script fonts for body copy, forms, or essential information.
- Do not sacrifice readability to appear luxurious.

Required variants:

- Full-colour or primary version

- Light version for dark or photographic backgrounds

## 13. Photography Direction

### Primary style

- Authentic property and destination photography
- Natural or golden-hour light
- Cool highland atmosphere balanced by warm interiors
- Deep visual layers: architecture, landscape, mist, and mountain
- Human moments that feel observed rather than staged
- Premium but believable colour grading

### Essential subject groups

- Aerial property and mountain context
- Arrival and architecture
- Room interiors and balcony views
- Bed, bathroom, and room details
- Spa treatment rooms and genuine rituals
- La Kana Chapel exterior and interior
- Real weddings and celebrations with permission
- Family stays and facilities
- Dining and breakfast experiences
- Gardens, pathways, sunrise, mist, and evening atmosphere
- Nearby destination context where licensing permits

### Editing treatment

- Preserve the natural green and misty environment.
- Keep whites warm rather than blue.
- Use terracotta and wood as controlled warm notes.
- Avoid oversaturation and strong HDR.
- Preserve shadow detail in architecture.
- Do not remove weather and mist when they contribute to the highland character.
- Maintain realistic room dimensions and colours.

### Image rules

- Use real property images whenever making a factual promise.
- Do not use generic stock resort images to represent Susan Spa & Resort.
- Do not generate fictional rooms, views, spa treatments, people, weddings, or facilities.
- Label conceptual imagery clearly if it must be shown before construction.
- Store alt text based on actual image content, not keyword stuffing.
- Prepare separate desktop and mobile crops for critical hero images.

## 14. Iconography and Illustration

### Icon style

- Simple, calm, and functional
- Medium stroke weight
- Optically consistent
- Relevant to the actual facility or action
- Tested at small sizes

### Rules

- Do not use sparkles, robots, magic wands, or decorative AI-style symbols.
- Do not place an icon above every heading by default.
- Do not use emoji as interface decoration.
- Icons must clarify meaning, not fill empty space.
- Facility icons must use one coherent family.
- Custom botanical or architectural motifs may be developed only when grounded in the property identity.

Illustration is secondary to real photography. Use illustration only for maps, wayfinding, spa rituals, or destination information where it improves understanding.

## 15. Layout and Grid

### Desktop

- 12-column grid
- Content width: approximately 1200 to 1360 px
- Generous outer margins
- Editorial asymmetry is allowed when it supports content hierarchy
- Large photography may break the content grid deliberately

### Tablet

- 8-column grid
- Preserve hierarchy rather than compressing desktop layouts
- Booking controls must remain comfortable and readable

### Mobile

- 4-column grid
- Minimum side padding: 20 px
- Single-column flow for essential content
- Minimum interactive target: 44 by 44 px
- No horizontal overflow
- Avoid carousels for critical information
- Keep booking and contact actions easy to reach without obstructing content

### Spacing scale

Use a consistent base scale such as:

`4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128`

Spacing should create hierarchy. Do not apply the same vertical gap to every section.

---

## 16. Shape and Surface Language

### Border radius

Use restrained, consistent radii:

- Small controls: 6 to 8 px
- Cards and media: 10 to 16 px
- Pills only for real status, filters, or compact selectors

Do not make every button, image, form field, card, and badge pill-shaped.

### Borders

- Thin, warm neutral borders
- Use borders to separate information, not to decorate every surface
- Avoid double borders and glowing outlines

### Shadows

- Use only where elevation or interaction requires it
- Keep most content surfaces flat
- Booking panels, dialogs, and floating navigation may use a restrained shadow
- Avoid large soft shadows on every card

### Decorative treatments

- No background grid
- No floating gradient orbs
- No full-page glow
- No excessive glassmorphism
- No texture unless it reflects a real material or approved brand motif

## 17. Component Direction

### Navigation

Desktop navigation should remain concise and reflect only existing destinations.

Recommended information architecture:

- Stay
- Spa
- Weddings
- Experiences
- Dining, if verified and content-ready
- Gallery
- Contact

Primary navigation action:

- **Check availability** or **Book your stay**

Do not include a navigation item that leads nowhere. If an area lacks complete content, omit it until ready.

### Hero

The hero should communicate the mountain setting immediately.

Requirements:

- One strong property image or a carefully selected short video
- One headline
- One supporting statement
- One primary conversion action
- Optional secondary exploratory action
- Booking interaction that does not obscure the photography

Do not use autoplay audio, heavy parallax, artificial status badges, generic claims, or a rotating carousel with several competing messages.

### Booking bar

Recommended fields:

- Arrival date
- Departure date
- Guests or rooms when supported
- Promo code as secondary or collapsible
- Check availability

Requirements:

- Works with keyboard
- Clear validation
- Correct date constraints
- Visible loading and error states
- Mobile layout designed independently
- Never display fabricated availability or pricing

### Room cards

Every room card should prioritise:

- Real image
- Room name
- Guest capacity
- Bed configuration
- Size when verified
- One or two meaningful differentiators
- Starting rate only when live and accurate
- View room details

Avoid identical cards filled with generic descriptions. Comparison information must be scannable.

### Room detail pages

Recommended content order:

1. Room name and strongest visual
2. Live availability or booking action
3. Capacity, size, bed, and view
4. Image gallery
5. Room description
6. Amenities and inclusions
7. Policies and practical information
8. Related room alternatives
9. Final booking action

### Spa section

Use calm editorial composition with real treatment photography.

Show only verified:

- Treatment name
- Duration
- Price
- Purpose or experience description
- Contraindications when applicable
- Booking process
- Operating hours

Avoid medical claims unless legally approved and supported.

### Weddings and La Kana Chapel

This section should combine emotion with practical confidence.

Recommended content:

- Real venue photography
- Venue story and setting
- Ceremony and reception options
- Verified capacity and configurations
- Package inclusions
- Real wedding gallery
- Planning support
- Dedicated enquiry form

Do not fabricate weddings, couples, reviews, capacity, or package values.

### Enquiry forms

Keep fields proportional to the decision.

For wedding and event enquiries, suitable fields may include:

- Name
- Contact information
- Preferred date
- Estimated guests
- Event type
- Accommodation requirement
- Message

Requirements:

- Inline validation
- Clear privacy statement
- Loading, success, and error states
- Confirmation of what happens next
- No unnecessary personal data collection

### Footer

Build the footer around real guest needs, not a generic four-column template.

Include:

- Official address
- Verified telephone and WhatsApp
- Official email
- Directions
- Essential policies and legal pages
- Social media
- Booking action

## 20. Responsive Behaviour

### Mobile priorities

1. Property identity
2. Availability and booking
3. Room decision support
4. Contact and directions
5. Wedding or spa enquiry

### Rules

- Provide mobile-specific hero crops.
- Preserve the resort buildings as the focal point in aerial photographs.
- Do not shrink desktop text over a mobile image.
- Avoid horizontally scrolling room cards for essential comparison.
- Keep sticky booking actions compact and dismissible when appropriate.

- Prevent fixed WhatsApp and booking buttons from overlapping content or each other.

## 21. Motion Direction

Motion should support orientation and feedback.

Allowed:

- Subtle navigation transitions
- Image fade or mask reveal when it does not delay content
- Button and link feedback
- Accordion transitions
- Gallery transitions
- Booking status feedback

Avoid:

- Constant floating animations
- Pulsing decorative dots
- Parallax that creates motion sickness
- Scroll hijacking
- Long entrance sequences
- Animation on every card
- Autoplay carousels with important content

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 22. Accessibility

Minimum requirements:

- WCAG 2.2 AA target
- Normal text contrast of at least 4.5:1
- Large text contrast of at least 3:1
- Keyboard-operable controls
- Visible focus states
- Correct semantic heading order
- Labels for every form field
- Useful alternative text for meaningful images
- Empty alt text for decorative images
- Captions or transcripts for meaningful video
- Error messages that explain how to fix the issue
- Dialogs closable with Escape
- No information communicated by colour alone
- Tap targets of at least 44 by 44 px

Photography and elegance must never reduce readability.

---

## 23. Performance

The site must feel calm because it responds quickly, not because it hides content behind animation.

Targets:

- Optimise Core Web Vitals
- Use responsive images and modern formats
- Prioritise the hero image responsibly
- Lazy-load non-critical media
- Avoid oversized video on mobile
- Subset and self-host fonts where licensing permits
- Avoid unnecessary JavaScript for visual effects
- Preserve booking and contact functions under slower mobile connections

A visually strong site that loads slowly does not support the brand promise.

## 26. Design Decisions and Their Reasons

- **Nature-led photography:** the mountain setting is a genuine differentiator and should do more work than decoration.
- **Editorial serif headlines:** they support an established, romantic hospitality character without relying on ornamental effects.
- **Humanist sans-serif body text:** operational details and booking tasks need strong readability.
- **Forest and ivory foundation:** they connect landscape and architecture while providing calm, high-contrast surfaces.
- **Terracotta accent:** it references the roofscape and adds warmth without competing with photography.
- **Moderate rhythm:** different guest journeys need compositional variety, but the site must remain easy to navigate.
- **Low motion:** the experience should feel restful and performant.
- **Clear conversion hierarchy:** direct booking and qualified enquiries are central business outcomes.
- **Real imagery and verified content:** trust is more valuable than decorative claims.

---

## 27. Anti-Patterns

Do not use:

- Generic blue-purple gradients
- Background grids or glowing orbs
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Sparkle icons or decorative emoji
- A badge above every headline
- Identical feature cards for every content type
- A default bento grid without content justification
- Fake room rates, testimonials, statistics, or awards
- Dead buttons and empty navigation destinations
- Autoplay hero sliders with competing messages
- Generic stock resort imagery presented as the property
- Script fonts for essential content
- Low-contrast grey-on-grey text
- Three equal pricing cards unless the real offer has exactly three tiers
- A generic four-column corporate footer
- Dark mode merely to appear premium
- A copied Bali, Santorini, Vercel, or luxury-template aesthetic

## 28. Required UI States

Every data-dependent interface must include:

- Loading state
- Empty state
- Error state
- Success state when an action completes

Required for:

- Availability search
- Booking engine hand-off
- Room rates
- Promo-code validation
- Gallery loading
- Contact forms
- Wedding and event enquiries
- Spa enquiries
- Map or directions integration

## 29. Pre-Launch Quality Gate

Before launch, verify:

### Brand

- The design reflects the highland, wellness, and celebration positioning.
- Photography is authentic and properly licensed.
- Logo usage is approved.
- Proposed colours are reconciled with official brand assets.

### Content

- Every room name, capacity, amenity, rate, and policy is verified.
- Spa and wedding information is approved.
- No claim, number, award, or testimonial is fabricated.
- Indonesian and English content are reviewed by a human.

### Function

- Every navigation item reaches a real destination.
- Every button performs its stated action.
- Booking dates and validation work.
- Forms provide loading, error, and success feedback.
- WhatsApp, phone, email, and map links use verified destinations.
- Analytics and conversion events are tested.

### Accessibility

- Contrast passes WCAG AA.
- Keyboard navigation works in visual order.
- Focus indicators are visible.
- Forms have labels and useful errors.
- Reduced motion is respected.

### Responsive quality

- No horizontal overflow.
- Images use deliberate mobile crops.
- Booking tools remain usable on small screens.
- Floating contact tools do not cover content.
- Navigation works across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

### Performance and SEO

- Production build succeeds.
- Console has no unresolved errors.
- Core Web Vitals are reviewed.
- Metadata and canonical URLs are correct.
- Redirects are tested.
- Structured data matches visible content.

A release fails if any critical booking, accessibility, trust, or mobile requirement fails.
